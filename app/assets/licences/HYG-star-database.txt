Changes made for Candra: only the stars of magnitude 6.5 or brighter
(the naked-eye sky) are kept, the Sun is left out, and of each star only
its J2000 right ascension (in degrees) and declination, magnitude, colour
index, constellation, Bayer and Flamsteed designations, Hipparcos and
Bright Star numbers and proper name are kept. The resulting star table,
assets/stars/hyg_v44_naked_eye.csv, is itself licensed under CC BY-SA
4.0. This licence covers that star table only, not the rest of the app.
