OpenNGC, a licence-friendly NGC/IC objects database
Mattia Verga, https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC

Used by Candra: the galaxies of the star map (database_files/NGC.csv and
addendum.csv, read 16 October 2026). Changed: kept to galaxies of visual
magnitude 10 or brighter and every Messier galaxy; name, designations,
J2000 place, magnitude, size and constellation.
