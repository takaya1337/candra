Wilayah Indonesia (cahya dsn)
https://github.com/cahyadsn/wilayah

Used by Candra: the official names and coordinates of Indonesia's
provinces, kabupaten and kota, and their boundaries (to place each
kecamatan), after Kepmendagri No 300.2.2-2138 Tahun 2025, as its header states.
